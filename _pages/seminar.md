## 讨论班简介 / About

本讨论班聚焦于**偏微分方程（PDE）的数学分析与数值方法**，特别关注其与**复杂系统**研究中的若干核心课题的交叉：

- 界面动力学与相场模型（Allen–Cahn、Cahn–Hilliard、phase-field、crystal growth）
- 不可压缩流体与表面准地转方程（Euler、Navier–Stokes、SQG）
- 自由边界问题与隐式边界值问题（oxygen diffusion、Stefan-type）
- 分数阶与非局域算子（fractional Laplacian、Oldroyd-B、Oldroyd-type）
- 色散与波动方程（Klein–Gordon–Zakharov、quasilinear wave）
- 高维 PDE 的 AI 求解方法（PINN、neural ODE、deep BSDE、Besov-based IBP）

This seminar focuses on the **mathematical analysis and numerical methods for PDEs**, with particular emphasis on their interactions with several core topics in **complex systems** research:

- Interface dynamics and phase-field models (Allen–Cahn, Cahn–Hilliard, phase-field, crystal growth)
- Incompressible fluids and surface quasi-geostrophic equations (Euler, Navier–Stokes, SQG)
- Free boundary and implicit boundary value problems (oxygen diffusion, Stefan-type)
- Fractional and nonlocal operators (fractional Laplacian, Oldroyd-B, Oldroyd-type)
- Dispersive and wave equations (Klein–Gordon–Zakharov, quasilinear wave)
- AI methods for high-dimensional PDEs (PINN, neural ODE, deep BSDE, Besov-based IBP)
